Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?
No. MovieClue is fully anonymous — there's no sign-up, no email, and no account required.
Why didn't MovieClue find my movie?
Try adding more specific details — a character name, a quote, the approximate time period, or the setting. More detail generally produces better matches.
Is there a limit to how many searches I can run?
Yes, there's a daily search limit to keep the service available and fair for everyone. It resets each day.
Where is my search history stored?
Only on your own device. It's never uploaded anywhere, and you can clear it any time from Settings.
How do I delete my data?
Open Settings in the app and use "Clear All Local Data." Since there's no account, this removes everything the app has stored on your device.
