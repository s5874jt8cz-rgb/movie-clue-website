Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

MovieClue is an anonymous app. There is no account, no email address, and no sign-in required to use it.

What stays on your device

Your search history and saved favourites are stored only in local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers and never leave your device unless you choose to share a result yourself. You can clear this data at any time from the app's Settings screen.

What is sent to our servers

When you run a search, two things are sent to our server so it can process your request:

  • Your description text — used only to identify the movie or TV show you're describing. It is passed to AI language-model providers and movie/TV databases (such as TMDb, OMDb, and Wikidata) to generate your results. We do not keep a permanent, readable copy of your description; only a one-way cryptographic hash of it is retained briefly, solely to detect duplicate/repeated searches and prevent abuse.
  • An anonymous installation identifier — a random ID generated on your device, not tied to your name, email, or any account. It is used only to enforce a daily search limit, prevent abuse, and (if you ever purchase a subscription) track entitlement status. It is not an advertising identifier and is never shared with advertising networks.

Network-level abuse protection

To prevent abuse across many installations, our server may compute a one-way cryptographic hash of coarse, non-identifying network information (such as a truncated user-agent string). The raw information itself is never stored — only the hash — and it cannot be reversed to reveal your IP address or device details.

Error diagnostics

If the app encounters an unexpected error, a small diagnostic report may be sent to help us fix bugs. This report contains only a general error category, a short technical message, and which screen it happened on — it never contains your search text or any personal information. These reports are automatically deleted after a maximum of 30 days and are accessible only to the app's administrators.

What we don't do

  • We don't require or collect your name, email address, or any account information.
  • We don't use advertising trackers or share data with ad networks.
  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your choices

You can clear your locally stored search history and favourites at any time from the app's Settings screen. Because MovieClue has no accounts, there is no separate "account" to delete — clearing local data removes everything the app has stored about your usage on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
